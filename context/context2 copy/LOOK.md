## Mood

*Three or four words for how it should feel.*

> Retro comic, tactile print, snappy, kinetic.

## Palette

*Colors, or a description of them.*

> Warm newsprint paper background (`#F4EBD9`), chunky ink black outlines (`#1E1B1E`), with distinctive world accents:
> - **World 1 (Print Shop):** Vermilion red (`#E94B35`) and mustard yellow (`#E9B949`) with black ink drop and silver scissors.
> - **World 2 (The Garden):** Terracotta earth (`#C86D51`), soft leaf green (`#76A035`), pink worm, and robin/jay blue feathers.
> - **World 3 (Midnight Sky):** Deep midnight blue (`#18233C`), pale lunar cream (`#FFF4D2`), and fiery gold sun bursts (`#F7B32B`). The moon avatar should be cream colored.
