# Look

How the game looks and sounds. References help Claude more than adjectives.

## Mood

*Three or four words for how it should feel.*

Example: "Foggy, quiet, lonely, warm light."

>

## Palette

*Colors, or a description of them.*

Example: "Grey-blue sea, near-black sky, one warm yellow for the beam."

>

## References

*Games, films, art, or photos that capture it. Put images and links in `context/references/` and say here what each one is for.*

Example: "The fog in *Limbo*; Hopper's lighthouse paintings."

>
