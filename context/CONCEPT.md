# Concept

Your game, in your words. Claude builds from this file, so what you write here steers everything.
Examples come from a different imaginary game. Don't copy them; they show the size of an answer.

## Pitch

*In one or two sentences, what is the game?*

Example: "You are a lighthouse keeper guiding ships through fog by sweeping a beam across the sea."

>

## Player fantasy

*What should the player feel like, or get to be?*

Example: "Calm but responsible: the only light in a dark storm."

>

## Why it's yours

*Where did this idea come from? What about it do you care about?*

Example: "My grandfather fished off the coast and talked about fog more than storms."

>
