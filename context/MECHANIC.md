# Mechanic

How the game plays. Jam rule: the whole game is built around **one action the player repeats** (jump, dodge, run, aim, time a tap).

## The one action

*What is the single action the player repeats, and what makes doing it again feel good?*

> Run forward through an unfinished comic panel, using `Space` to jump/stomp on monster heads or `/` to slash through incoming threats with a satisfying sword swing and sound effect.

## 500 times

*Would you do this action 500 times? What's different each time?*

> Yes: monsters change behavior and speed as the panels ramp from 1 to 8, alternating between low ground crawlers you must stomp or jump over, and flying threats you must duck under or slash mid-air.

## The worst 30 seconds

*Picture the most boring or frustrating 30 seconds of this game. What happens?*

> Slashing misses due to poor timing, or waiting too long on an empty stretch with no incoming obstacles.

## Controls

*Which keys or mouse actions, and what does each do?*

> - `Left` / `Right` arrows (or `A` / `D`): Move forward and backward across the panel.
> - `Space`: Jump (can land on top of monsters to stomp and crush them).
> - `/`: Slash attack (swings a curved comic arc in front of the player, accompanied by an 8-bit sword audio effect).

## Win and lose

*How does a round end? What counts as doing well?*

> - **Win:** Clear all 8 escalating panels in a run to ink the entire issue and reveal the completed 8-panel comic strip with total score.
> - **Lose:** Taking a direct unblocked hit from a monster halts the run at that panel with an unfinished crossed-out sketch. Press `R` or Space to instantly retry.
