# References

Drop **images and links only** that show what your game should look, move, or feel like: screenshots of games, film stills, photos, sketches (`.png`, `.jpg`, `.gif`, `.webp`), plus a `links.md` with URLs to videos or pages. No video files: link to them instead.

Name files so they say why they're here: `tron-light-trails.png`, `my-sketch-cat.jpg`. In `links.md`, one line per link saying why it's there.

Claude looks at these before it builds. They are for reference only: they stay in this folder, never ship to the gallery, and their art is never copied into your game.
