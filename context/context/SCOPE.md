# Scope

You have the jam (11:00 AM to 4:00 PM). Decide now what's in, so cutting later is easy.

## In (must exist to call it a game)

*The smallest version someone can play start to finish.*

> - One responsive player entity with run, jump (gravity), stomp collision, and slash attack (`/`) with synthesized Web Audio sound effect.
> - 3 world themes randomly chosen on play:
>   1. Ink Drop vs Scissors & Erasers
>   2. The Worm vs Robins & Blue Jays
>   3. Crescent Moon vs Shooting Stars & Suns
> - 8 panels per run with increasing monster spawn rate and speed.
> - Panel clear mechanic: clearing monsters inks the panel and transitions to the next panel.
> - End-of-game screen: displays all 8 cleared panels together as a completed comic strip.
> - Game over and instant restart.

## Out (not this jam)

*Things you like but are leaving out on purpose.*

> - Branching dialogue trees or multi-path story choices.
> - Inventory, weapon upgrades, or coin shops.
> - Complex external 3D asset loaders or heavyweight physics engines.
> - Multiplayer or backend database accounts.

## Stretch (only if time is left)

*Next things to add once the In list works.*

> - Screen shake and comic punch-in on slash hit.
> - Downloadable PNG button for the completed 8-panel comic strip.
> - High score and combo counter.
