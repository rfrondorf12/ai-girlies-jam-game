# Concept

Your game, in your words. Claude builds from this file, so what you write here steers everything.

## Pitch

*In one or two sentences, what is the game?*

> You are an abstract hero trapped inside an unfinished comic book, leaping, stomping, and slashing through 8 escalating panels of contradictory monsters to ink and assemble a complete comic strip issue.

## Player fantasy

*What should the player feel like, or get to be?*

> A snappy, bold comic protagonist bringing a printed world to life with every leap and slash, turning rough pencil sketches into finished, inked comic art.

## Why it's yours

*Where did this idea come from? What about it do you care about?*

> Inspired by vintage printed comic strips, risograph print defects, and abstract geometric characters. We wanted the core action of surviving and fighting to visibly draw and complete the comic strip panel by panel.
