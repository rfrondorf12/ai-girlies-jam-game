- Characters: `assets/characters/*.glb` are v2, generated by `blender/make_characters_v2.py` (Blender 4.2, built from code; CC0 / your own).
- Cars, barricades, cones, fences, debris, skyline, sky, moon, road/sidewalk textures, particles, and the grading shader are all built in code inside `main.js`. Nothing is downloaded.
- Fonts: Bungee and Rubik from Google Fonts (SIL OFL), loaded from the CDN.
